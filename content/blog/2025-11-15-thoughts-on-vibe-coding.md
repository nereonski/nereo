2025-11-15 - Thoughts on vibe coding

Vibe coding completely transformed how I approach software, even though I'm not yet a professional developer - just someone learning how to code, hoping to be one someday. My coding journey began with curiosity and a bit of hesitation, but these AI-powered tools made the leap surprisingly accessible.

Vibe coding is about describing the outcome and feeling I want from my app or script, then letting AI handle the technical details. Admittedly, it's made me a little lazy with the fundamentals, as I sometimes lean on AI for the heavy lifting. But paradoxically, it's exactly this convenience that pushed me into coding in the first place. I can quickly build workable prototypes - even as a total beginner - which keeps me motivated and eager to learn more.

Of course, it's not perfect. Sometimes the AI misses subtle instructions or creates buggy code, and I have to step in and problem-solve myself. But I love how vibe coding reduces friction and opens the door for non-developers. The process feels creative, fast, and inclusive, and everyone - regardless of background - has a chance to build something real.

For me, vibe coding turned coding from a daunting skill into an experiment-filled adventure that's practical, fun, and full of possibility.
