SANDBOX / EXPERIMENT 001

Space Game.

Canvas · vanilla JS · mobile-friendly controls

A small browser shooter. Endless waves, power-ups, and one more round. Built with curiosity, vanilla JavaScript, and a little help from AI.

Your browser needs Canvas support to play Space Game.
↑↓ ←→ or WASD Move
SPACE Shoot
P Pause
R Restart
or F Fullscreen
Exit

Choose Start, or focus the playfield and press Space. On touchscreens, use the joystick and shoot button.
